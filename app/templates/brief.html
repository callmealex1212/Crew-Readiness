04What this brief cannot tell you
  • It compares each person with their own earlier sessions on a typed checklist. It says nothing about why a pattern changed: a new keyboard, gloves, a distraction or a bad day look the same.
  • It is not a medical assessment and does not say anyone is fit or unfit. It never assigns or clears anyone.
  • If a person reports feeling unwell, that is a matter for crew medical support, whatever this brief shows.
  • No typed text or keystroke content was used. Only timing numbers.
  • This demonstration uses a fictional crew and simulated data. It has not been tested with real crews.
