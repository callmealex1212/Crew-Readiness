Attention

Optional attention check

A short reaction-time task offered after a YELLOW or RED advisory. Illustrative only. It adds context for a human reviewer and is not a medical test.

01Reaction time
Press Start. When this box lights up, click it or press Space as fast as you can.

02How to use it

The wait changes from trial to trial so the signal is not predictable. A response before the signal, or within 100 ms of it, restarts that trial. A missed signal is recorded after 1.5 seconds. This is context for a human reviewer, not a medical assessment.
