01Reaction time

Press Start. When this box lights up, click it or press Space as fast as you can.
02How it works
  • Ten trials. Each one waits a random 1.5 to 4 seconds, so the rhythm cannot be anticipated, then lights up.
  • Responding before the light, or less than 100 ms after it (too fast to be a reaction to it), counts as too early and restarts that trial's wait.
  • No response within 1.5 seconds of the light counts as a miss.
  • Only four numbers are stored: the median reaction time of valid trials, the trial count, and the too-early and missed counts. Escape cancels a run without sending anything.
  • Illustrative reaction-time check. Not a medical test. It is context for a human reviewer and has no threshold or score.
